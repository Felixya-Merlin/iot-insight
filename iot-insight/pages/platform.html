Platform

Smart infrastructure for connected data

IoT Insight brings together device data collection, storage, analysis, alerts, and reporting in one streamlined product for operational teams.

Data SourceThingSpeak channels, smart sensors, and connected industrial devices feed live readings into the system.
Data ImportUpload CSV data or connect APIs to bring structured readings into your cloud data workflow.
Cloud StorageStore device readings in Firebase with organized parameter and device-level records.
ProcessingValidate and normalize raw inputs before analysis to keep dashboards accurate and dependable.
AnalysisInspect trends, averages, variations, and time-based behavior across all connected assets.
DetectionTrigger alerts when sensor values drift from expected patterns or thresholds are crossed.
VisualizationRender live charts, health indicators, and dashboard summaries for instant operational insight.
ReportGenerate structured summaries and data views that support decisions, reviews, and daily reporting needs.
